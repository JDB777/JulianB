Observation log

What made the sky do that

A running record of the sunsets worth photographing from one stretch of beach, set against what the atmosphere was measurably doing at the time. Six evenings so far. Every one of them had a clear slot at the western horizon; on the three with weather data retrieved, the marine layer's edge was crossing the beach within an hour or two of sunset.
